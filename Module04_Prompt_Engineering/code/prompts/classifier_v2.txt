billing - payments, charges, invoices, refunds, subscription plans
technical - the website or app is broken, slow or showing an error
account - login, password, email address, profile details, deleting an account
content - mistakes or problems in course material: lessons, quizzes, subtitles
other - anything else, such as praise, feature requests or general feedback

Reply with the label only, in lowercase, with no other text.
