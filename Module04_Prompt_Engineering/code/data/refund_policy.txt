Nimbus Learning - Refund Policy (fictional, for training use)
Last updated: 1 August 2026

1. Course purchases
You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if you have watched
less than 30% of the course. Requests made after 14 days, or after watching 30%
or more of the course, are not eligible for a refund.

2. Pro subscription
Monthly Pro subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from Account > Plan.
Cancellation stops the next payment. We do not refund the current month,
except when a payment was taken twice by mistake, in which case the duplicate
payment is refunded in full.

3. How to ask for a refund
Go to Account > Orders > Request refund, choose the order and give a reason.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

4. Team plans
Team plans of 10 seats or more are covered by the team contract, not by this
policy. Contact your account manager.
