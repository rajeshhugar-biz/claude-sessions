NIMBUS LEARNING PVT LTD — EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK (fictional sample for training)

1. Working hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00, when all employees should be reachable. Flexible start times between 8:00 and 10:30 are allowed with manager approval.

2. Leave policy
Employees receive 12 casual leaves, 12 sick leaves and 18 earned leaves per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward. Earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 45 days. Sick leave of more than two consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Leave requests must be raised in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance, except for sick leave.

3. Public holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays each year. The list is published in December for the following year. Employees may swap up to 2 public holidays for regional festivals with manager approval.

4. Notice period
The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation. Senior managers and above have a 90-day notice period. Notice may be shortened by mutual agreement or by paying salary in lieu of notice.

5. Probation
New employees serve a 6-month probation period. Performance is reviewed at 3 months and at 6 months. Probation may be extended once by up to 3 months.

6. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR. Employees working remotely must be available during core hours and use the company VPN.

7. Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the travel desk. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Hotel limits are INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere. Claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts.

8. Equipment
Employees receive a laptop and headset. Equipment must be returned on the last working day. Loss or damage must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

9. Code of conduct
Employees must treat colleagues with respect, avoid conflicts of interest, protect confidential information and follow all laws. Gifts above INR 2,000 in value from clients or vendors must be declared.

10. Data protection
Customer and employee personal data may only be used for the purpose it was collected for. Personal data must never be pasted into unapproved AI tools. Suspected data breaches must be reported to the security team immediately.

11. Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses and certifications. Certification exam fees are reimbursed on passing. Employees who use more than INR 25,000 in a year agree to stay 12 months or repay a pro-rated amount.

12. Performance reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Ratings are on a 5-point scale. Salary revisions are effective from April 1.

13. Grievances
Employees may raise grievances with their manager, HR or the anonymous ethics hotline. HR acknowledges grievances within 2 working days and aims to resolve them within 15 working days.

14. Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid leave, which can be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

15. Referral bonus
Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and completes 90 days receive a referral bonus of INR 25,000, or INR 50,000 for senior roles.
