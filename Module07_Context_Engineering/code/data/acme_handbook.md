# Acme Home Appliances - Customer Support Handbook (fictional)

This handbook is invented for the Claude Certified Developer course. Acme Home
Appliances is not a real company. Agents use it to answer customer questions
about orders, deliveries, returns, warranties and service visits.

## Order status

Every order has an ID in the form ORD-12345. Agents can look up the status in the
order system. The possible statuses are placed, packed, shipped, out for delivery,
delivered and cancelled. If an order has been in the packed state for more than
three working days, raise a priority ticket with the warehouse team.

Customers can change the delivery address only while the order is placed or
packed. Once an order is shipped, the address cannot be changed and the customer
must refuse the delivery and place a new order.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in metro cities and 5 to 8 working
days elsewhere. Express delivery is available in Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru,
Hyderabad, Chennai and Delhi NCR and takes 1 to 2 working days. Express delivery
costs 199 rupees per order and is free for orders above 15,000 rupees.

Large appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners are
delivered by our own installation partners. The customer receives an SMS with a
two-hour delivery window on the morning of delivery.

## Returns and refunds

Small appliances can be returned within 10 days of delivery if they are unused
and in the original packaging. Large appliances can be returned within 7 days
only if they are damaged on arrival or do not match the order.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. UPI and card refunds take 5 to 7
working days after the return is picked up. Cash on delivery orders are refunded
by bank transfer within 10 working days after the customer shares bank details
through the secure form. Agents must never collect bank details over chat.

A restocking fee of 10 percent applies to opened small appliances returned for a
change of mind. There is no restocking fee for damaged or wrong items.

## Warranty

All Acme products carry a one-year manufacturer warranty from the date of
delivery. Compressors in refrigerators and air conditioners carry a ten-year
warranty. Motors in washing machines carry a five-year warranty.

The warranty does not cover damage from voltage fluctuation, water damage,
physical damage, or repairs done by anyone other than an authorised Acme service
partner. Customers can buy an extended warranty of one or two extra years within
90 days of delivery.

## Service visits

Customers can book a service visit through the app, the website or by calling
support. Visits inside warranty are free. Outside warranty, the visit fee is 399
rupees, and parts are charged separately at list price.

Service partners visit within 48 hours in metro cities and within 72 hours
elsewhere. If a product needs the same repair three times within the warranty
period, the customer is eligible for a replacement unit.

## Installation

Installation is free for air conditioners, washing machines, dishwashers and
chimneys. It is booked automatically when the product is delivered. Wall-mount
kits for televisions cost 1,499 rupees. Copper pipe beyond the standard 3 metres
for air conditioners is charged at 650 rupees per metre.

## Escalation

If a customer is unhappy with the resolution, the agent offers an escalation to a
team lead. Team leads respond within one working day. Complaints about safety,
such as sparks, burning smells or electric shocks, are escalated immediately to
the safety desk, and the agent tells the customer to switch off and unplug the
product.

## Payments and invoices

Acme accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and cash on delivery for
orders up to 50,000 rupees. No-cost EMI is available on cards from partner banks
for orders above 10,000 rupees. GST invoices are emailed when the order ships and
can be downloaded from the orders page.

## Privacy rules for agents

Agents may confirm the last four digits of a phone number but must never read out
a full phone number, address or email to a caller who has not been verified.
Verification means the caller confirms the order ID and the registered PIN code.
Never paste customer personal data into internal chat tools.
